# Social Posts — Thinking About Policing

Copy-paste-ready posts to push articles on **LinkedIn** (your senior-leader /
policymaker audience) and **X** (reach, journalists). Every link is the live
canonical URL.

## How to use this for SEO (and how it shows up in your before/after)
- Social links are **`nofollow`** — they don't pass direct ranking power — but
  they drive the **traffic, repeat visits and backlinks** that *do* move
  rankings, and they get new articles **discovered and indexed faster**.
- It also makes the **referrer breakdown in GoatCounter** meaningful: you'll see
  LinkedIn / X / t.co show up, so you can separate "SEO traffic" from "social
  traffic" when you compare before vs after.
- **Cadence that works:** 3–4 posts/week. Lead with the idea, not the link.
  Re-share evergreen pieces every 6–8 weeks with a fresh opening line.
- **LinkedIn tip:** put the link in the **first comment** (or accept it inline)
  — either way, the new `og-default.png` / hero image now renders a proper
  preview card, which lifts click-through.
- **Hashtags:** 3–5 on LinkedIn, 1–2 on X. Suggested set:
  `#Policing #EvidenceBasedPolicing #PublicPolicy #PolicingByConsent #PoliceLeadership`
