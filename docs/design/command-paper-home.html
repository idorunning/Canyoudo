Lead paper

Can an Electric Car Catch a Criminal?

The physics, the payload and the pursuit — a guide for the people signing the fleet forms.

British policing has promised to electrify a fleet that is four-fifths diesel. The pursuit car, the armed response vehicle and the 3am rural shift are governed by physics that a procurement target cannot waive.

What follows is not an argument for or against the transition. It is an attempt to do three things: match the car to the role it is actually asked to perform, fund the charging before the vehicle, and stop a single successful demonstration from writing fleet policy for a decade.

Two interactive tools sit inside the paper — a pursuit simulator that shows where battery mass helps and where it does not, and an ARV loadout calculator that takes payload seriously.
