Questions, challenges, corrections, and different perspectives are all welcome — disagreement done well is how the writing gets better. If you have spotted an error, say so plainly and I will fix it and note the correction.

Comments on each article are also open and need no login, so if your point belongs in public, leave it there and others can join in.

## A note on views

Everything on this site is written in a personal capacity. The views are mine alone and do not represent Sussex Police, any other employer, or any organisation I am associated with. Anything operational or concerning a live policing matter should go through official channels, not this site.
