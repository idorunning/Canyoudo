Comments on each article are also open and need no login, so if your point belongs in public, feel free to post it there and others can join in.

## A note on views

Everything on this site is written in a personal capacity. The views are mine alone and do not represent any Police organisation, any other employer, or any organisation I am associated with. Anything operational or concerning a live policing matter should go through official channels, not this site.
