## Why the change

The old WordPress site did its job, but it never quite felt like mine. Editing was clunky. The design fought what I wanted to write. And every time I went to publish something, I'd spend ten minutes wrestling with the editor before I got to the writing.

The new site is built on Astro. Each article is a markdown file, written in VS Code, pushed to GitHub. Netlify builds and deploys automatically. The whole thing is faster, cleaner, and entirely under my control.

## What's on here

Three sections. **Police Policy** covers the operational and organisational side of the job — misconduct, wellbeing, use of force, neighbourhood policing. **Public Policy** is wider, taking in criminal justice, social policy, and the political context. **Other** is for everything that doesn't fit, including the Somerton Man pieces.

The writing aims at senior leaders, policymakers, and frontline officers who want something more considered than the usual commentary.

## What's coming

I've a few drafts queued up. Early intervention in police misconduct. A piece on what we've learned (and haven't) from Operation Hampshire. Something on the use of force data nobody wants to look at properly.
